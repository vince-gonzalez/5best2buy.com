Halal Frozen & Prepared Meals FAQ
What makes a prepared meal halal, beyond the meat?

Everything in it has to clear: the meat must be properly slaughtered, and the sauces, thickeners, and flavorings can't contain pork gelatin, non-halal enzymes, or alcohol-based extracts. That's a much taller order than a raw cut, which only has to be slaughtered right. It's why a named certifier matters more on a frozen entree than on a plain steak.

Are Saffron Road and J&M independently owned?

Yes. Saffron Road is the brand of American Halal Company, founded and run by Adnan Durrani since 2010. J&M Halal Meals is made by J&M Food Products, a company that has produced shelf-stable meals since 1991. Neither is a conglomerate or private-equity roll-up, which is the whole reason they're on this shelf.

Do the shelf-stable meals really not need refrigeration?

Correct. J&M's meals are sealed in retort pouches and sterilized with heat, so an unopened pouch stays safe at room temperature for years without preservatives. That's the same technology behind military and travel rations. Once you open one, treat it like any cooked food and refrigerate leftovers.

How do these ship, and how do I prepare them?

J&M's shelf-stable pouches ship by regular ground with no cold chain, and you heat them in boiling water or a microwave in a few minutes. Saffron Road's frozen entrees ship cold and should go into the freezer on arrival, then heat from frozen in about five minutes. Check each package for exact times.
