Straight Answers
Lobster FAQ
How do I keep a live lobster before cooking?

Refrigerate it in the fridge (not on ice, not in fresh water, which kills it) covered with a damp towel or newspaper, and cook it within a day. It should still move when you pick it up. All three makers ship it live and cold, packed with gel packs or seaweed.

Are frozen lobster tails any good?

Yes, if they're flash-frozen fresh. A tail frozen at the peak can beat a 'fresh' lobster that sat in a warm tank for days. Cold-water (Maine/Canadian) tails are firmer and sweeter than warm-water tails — all of these are cold-water.

What's the most humane way to cook a lobster?

Chilling it in the freezer for 15-20 minutes first sedates it. Then most cooks either plunge it head-first into rapidly boiling salted water, or split the head lengthwise with a knife before cooking. Steaming (rather than boiling) keeps the meat a bit more tender.

Why is grocery-store lobster often mushy or off?

Lobster held too long in a crowded tank stops eating and its muscle breaks down, going soft and cottony. The fix is a short chain: buy from an outfit that ships within a day or two of the catch, which is exactly what a dock-direct Maine seller does.
